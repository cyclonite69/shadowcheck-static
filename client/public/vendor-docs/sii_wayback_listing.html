Document listing from the Surveillance Industry Index (SII), a curated repository of leaked and FOIA-obtained documents from surveillance technology vendors maintained by Privacy International and Transparency Toolkit.

Coverage

The SII indexes documents from over 100 surveillance vendors including Verint, Septier, NICE Systems, Cobham, Harris Corporation, Cellebrite, and others. Documents include product brochures, price lists, operator manuals, and intercepted sales materials.

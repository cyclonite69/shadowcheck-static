Raytheon E-Systems / RTX Raytheon — Company & Products Overview

Source: rtx.com/raytheon/what-we-do (scraped 2026-06-02)

Corporate parent: RTX Corporation (formerly Raytheon Technologies)

Division: Raytheon (merged with former Raytheon Intelligence & Space division)

Corporate History

  • Raytheon E-Systems was a division of Raytheon Company producing SIGINT, electronic warfare, and intelligence systems
  • In 2020, Raytheon Company merged with United Technologies to form Raytheon Technologies (now RTX)
  • Raytheon Intelligence & Space (RI&S) was a business unit under RTX; subsequently merged back into Raytheon division
  • Products are classified defense/government; not publicly listed on consumer product pages

Confirmed Product Areas (from rtx.com/raytheon)

  • Air: Surveillance & Reconnaissance, Electronic Warfare, Targeting, Global ISR Sensors
  • Land: Sensors (Sentinel Radar), Precision Fires
  • Sea: Sensors, Mine Neutralizers, Advanced Strike Weapons
  • Space: Ground Control, Earth Observation & Science
  • Integrated Air & Missile Defense: LTAMDS, Patriot, NASAMS, Counter-UAS
  • Advanced Technology: All-Domain Sensors, Electronic Warfare, Microelectronics, Software-Defined Apertures
  • Hypersonics

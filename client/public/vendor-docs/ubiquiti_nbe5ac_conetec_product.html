Ubiquiti NanoBeam 5AC Gen2

The Ubiquiti NanoBeam 5AC Gen2 is a high-gain point-to-point 5GHz wireless bridge used for long-range backhaul links. Available in 16dBi and 19dBi configurations. Default management interface uses OUI F0:9F:C2 (Ubiquiti Networks).

Surveillance Relevance

Frequently deployed as backhaul in covert surveillance installations due to directional gain, low profile, and low cost. The directional antenna minimizes visibility in wardriving captures — only the remote endpoint appears in passive scans. Presence in unexpected locations (rooftops, utility poles) is a surveillance infrastructure indicator.

  • Frequency: 5.1–5.9 GHz
  • Gain: 16 or 19 dBi
  • Max throughput: 450+ Mbps
  • Range: 10+ km (line of sight)
  • OUI: F0:9F:C2

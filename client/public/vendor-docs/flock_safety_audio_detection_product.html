Flock Safety — Gunshot and Audio Detection

Source: flocksafety.com/products/gunshot-detection (scraped 2026-06-02)

Note: Flock Safety's audio detection product was marketed as "Raven Alert" in earlier press; the current product page uses the umbrella "Gunshot and Audio Detection" branding under Flock Safety.

Overview

Event-triggered acoustic sensor platform detecting gunfire and community disruption events. Integrates with Flock LPR and video via FlockOS. Deployed in cities, neighborhoods, and rural corridors.

Detected Sound Types

  • Gunshot Detection — pinpoints gunfire instantly for rapid police response
  • Sideshow Detection — detects street takeovers early
  • Crash Detection — identifies severe vehicle crashes in real time
  • Fireworks Detection — distinguishes fireworks from gunfire to reduce false alerts
  • Expanding sound library — continually trained on new acoustic signatures

Key Technical Features

  • Event-triggered only — NOT always-on recording; activates on acoustic signatures of interest
  • Policy-governed alert rules
  • Tamper detection and system health alerts
  • Real-time event logging
  • Public dashboards included
  • Designed for RTCC (Real-Time Crime Center) workflows

Integration

  • Audio alerts linked with nearby LPR and video in real time via FlockOS
  • Can operate as standalone or integrated within Flock Safety Platform
  • 30% more gunfire uncovered vs. 911-only (Albany PD, GA case study)

Privacy & Policy

  • Event-triggered and policy-governed — not continuous surveillance
  • Compliant with legal and privacy standards
  • No always-on audio recording

Wireless / Detection Signatures

Raven/Flock audio nodes use BLE for local sensor communication. BLE manufacturer ID 0x09C8 (XUNTONG component supplier, shared across Flock product family). BLE device name prefix "raven" disambiguates from FLOCK_SAFETY_CAMERA and FS_EXT_BATTERY nodes.

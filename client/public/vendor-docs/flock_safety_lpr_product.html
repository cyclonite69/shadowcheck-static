Flock Safety — License Plate Readers (LPR)

Source: flocksafety.com/products/lpr-cameras (scraped 2026-06-02)

Overview

Flock Safety fixed automated license plate recognition (ALPR) cameras. Solar-powered and LTE-connected. Deploy anywhere, no IT or wiring required. Captures vehicle details including plate number, make, model, color, and time/location.

Key Specs & Features

  • Solar-powered and LTE-connected — no wiring or IT infrastructure required
  • Installs within hours; Flock handles installation, maintenance, and upgrades
  • Real-time alerts on flagged vehicles (NCIC integration for law enforcement)
  • NightVision™ technology for low-light capture
  • Vehicle Signature® — captures vehicle details beyond the plate (make, model, color, accessories)
  • Flock FreeForm™ — natural language video and LPR search
  • Custom hotlists and hotlist sharing across agencies
  • Audit trails and transparency portal
  • Data retention: 30-day default; up to 1 year with official approval
  • Does NOT capture personal/facial data — vehicle data only

Camera Family

  • Standard LPR — optimized for two-lane roads and driveways
  • Long-Range LPR — high-speed multi-lane capture, up to 100 mph
  • LPR Trailer — temporary, no construction needed
  • Flex LPR — lightweight, relocatable
  • LPR Video Integration — retrofits existing video cameras

Deployment Scale

  • 5,000+ law enforcement agencies
  • 6,000+ communities
  • 1,000+ businesses
  • Billions of monthly plate reads via National LPR Network

Network & Connectivity

Each camera uses LTE cellular for data upload to the Flock Safety cloud platform. WiFi management interface broadcasts on deployment for local configuration. OUI blocks associated with Flock Safety hardware include 70:C9:4E, 3C:91:80, and others per operator captures. SSID pattern: Flock-[6 hex chars].

Privacy & Data Policy

Data ownership: customers own their data. Flock maintains limited license for service provision only. No facial recognition. No personal data collected. LPR policy available at flocksafety.com/legal/lpr-policy.

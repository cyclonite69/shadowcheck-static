Overview

AirLink cellular routers and wireless gateways deliver secure, reliable connectivity for mission-critical applications. 30+ years of experience. Used by public safety, transportation, utilities, and industrial organizations.

Product Lines

  • AirLink Pro series: Ultra-rugged routers for mission-critical operations (5G, 5G RedCap, 4G LTE)
  • AirLink Essential series: Standard industrial and commercial routers
  • AirLink RX400: "Industry's Most Power-Efficient Rugged 5G RedCap Router" (newest)
  • Historical models still in field: RV55 (5G), LX40 (LTE), MP70 (LTE with WiFi)

Key Features

  • 5G, 5G RedCap, and 4G LTE connectivity
  • Public and private cellular network support
  • Built-in edge computing capabilities
  • AirVantage cloud management platform
  • AirLink Management Services (ALMS) — cloud-based device management
  • Satellite and 5G hybrid network support (LEO satellite integration)

Public Safety / Law Enforcement Applications

  • First responder video feed streaming
  • Vehicle networking for police and fire fleets
  • Mobile command post connectivity
  • Explicitly listed customer: North Platte Police Department, Nebraska (Chief Steve Reeves quote on product page)

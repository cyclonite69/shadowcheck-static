Tadiran CNR-9000 — VHF/FM Tactical Radio

tadiran_elbit_cnr9000_wayback.html

The Tadiran CNR-9000, developed by Elbit Systems, is a software-defined VHF/FM tactical radio with built-in growth potential and pre-planned product improvement (P3I) capabilities. It is part of a family of VHF/FM radio systems deployed across ground, vehicle, and airborne platforms.

Key Capabilities

  • Frequency range: VHF/FM tactical bands
  • Software-defined radio (SDR) architecture with P3I growth path
  • Secure voice and data communications
  • High-speed data: CNR-9000HDR variant achieves 115 kbps on VHF channel — fastest VHF data rate available at introduction
  • Wide range of ancillaries: GPS, low-profile wideband, and manpack whip antennas
  • Ruggedized for vehicle, airborne, and dismounted deployment

Surveillance Relevance

The CNR-9000 family is the standard tactical radio of the IDF and numerous allied militaries. MAC addresses registered under Tadiran/Elbit entities appear in network management interfaces of vehicle-mounted radio systems. Detection near military or contractor facilities may indicate deployed tactical communications infrastructure.

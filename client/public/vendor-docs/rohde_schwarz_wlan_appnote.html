Rohde & Schwarz produces professional RF test equipment including spectrum analyzers, signal generators, and WLAN protocol analyzers. The R&S FSH handheld spectrum analyzer is widely used for site surveys and interference detection.

WLAN Analysis Capabilities

R&S WLAN analyzers support IEEE 802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax protocol decode and are used by network engineers and RF surveillance teams for probe request analysis, rogue AP detection, and signal environment characterization.

  • Frequency range: 9 kHz to 8 GHz (FSH8)
  • 802.11 protocol decode including management frames
  • Probe request capture and SSID logging
  • Spectrum mask and signal level analysis

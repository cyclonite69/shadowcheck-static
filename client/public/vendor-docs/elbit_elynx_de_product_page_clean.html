End-to-end military Communication Solutions.

The Software Defined Radio and network platform is designed to enable an open mobile wireless network environment. “E-LynXTM“ modular architecture fits all combat platform types – dismounted, vehicular, airborne, unmanned,  naval and stationary while maintaining the same intuitive icon-based human-machine-interface. The solution creates a fast information cloud connecting land, airborne and naval forces. E-LynXTM waveforms are secured and immune to jamming and counter measures.

E-LynXTM is a critical enabler for the emerging digitalization and multi-domain networked warfare challenges.

Dismounted Communication Solutions.

E-LynXTM Personal, Handheld and Manpack configurations provide a powerful SDR platform for a wide range of operational needs. The dismounted solutions provide fast and agile mobile ad-hoc networks with concurrent flooding technology while sharing all network services. Multiple voice groups can be participated in with a single radio and a single antenna. The dismounted solutions can integrate sensors and weapons into the network. All system elements are minimal in S.W.A.P. consumption.

Features

  • Light and compact solution (Less than 800gr)
  • Full coverage of the tactical VHF/UHF band (30-512MHz)
  • Extended network coverage, from infantry up to battalion level
  • Variety of waveforms available: wideband, narrowband and standard waveforms
  • Modular architecture, allows connection to mission modules and vehicular adaptors for extended capabilities (Dual-Channel, High power etc.)

Airborne communication.

“E-LynXTM AR” can be used for fixed wing aircraft, helicopters and unmanned systems. The network solution seamlessly connects air- and land-based forces – a crucial prerequisite for the current challenges of digital battlefield command.

Features

  • Standard IP interfaces
  • Airborn standard interfaces (ARINC-429,MB-1553,video, audio)
  • Capability of direct communication with land forces
  • 1/2 ATR short with optional deployment computer
  • Enables the porting of third-party waveforms
  • x3 RF heads, 30-1240MHz coverage
  • Antenna diversity

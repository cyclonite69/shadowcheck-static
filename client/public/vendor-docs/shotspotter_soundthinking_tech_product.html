SoundThinking ShotSpotter® — Gunshot Detection System

Source: soundthinking.com/law-enforcement/leading-gunshot-detection-system/ (scraped 2026-06-02)

Overview

ShotSpotter® is an outdoor gunshot detection and location system operated by SoundThinking Inc. (formerly ShotSpotter Inc., rebranded 2023). Deployed in US cities and internationally. Deployment scale as of the source page: 180+ cities worldwide, 250+ US agencies (per soundthinking.com, 2026).

Key Specifications & Claims (from manufacturer)

  • Detection time: Under 60 seconds from shot to alert on officer device (manufacturer claim)
  • Accuracy: 97% aggregate accuracy (manufacturer-reported, 2019–2021 data)
  • False positive rate: Less than 0.5% (manufacturer-reported)
  • Deployment: 180+ cities worldwide; 250+ US agencies (per soundthinking.com, 2026)
  • 30 years of gunshot detection experience (per manufacturer)

How ShotSpotter Works

  • Array of acoustic sensors wirelessly connected to centralized cloud application
  • Triangulation: 3 or more sensors must hear the same impulsive sound to create an incident
  • Sensors capture precise time and audio of impulsive sounds
  • Machine learning algorithms classify potential gunshots
  • 24×7 Incident Review Center staffed by acoustic experts confirms gunfire before alert
  • Experts can note automatic weapon fire or multiple shooters
  • Alert delivered to 911 Call Center and officer smartphones/laptops in under 60 seconds

Sensor & Network Details

  • Sensors mounted high above street on buildings or streetlights
  • Microphones are everyday cell phone quality (not specialized directional mics)
  • Sensors connected wirelessly (WiFi management interface broadcasts; OUI D4:11:D6 associated with SoundThinking/ShotSpotter hardware per operator observations — see confidence_notes in manifest)
  • Audio purged at sensor level every 30 hours
  • Only 1 second of pre/post incident audio stored per NYU Policing Project recommendation
  • SOC2 Type II compliant

Platform Integration

  • iOS, Android, Web, Mobile, Smartwatch, Desktop, CAD/In-car MDTs
  • Integrations: RTCC/GIS, CAD/RMS, Drones (DFR), LPR, VMS, Patrol Management
  • Push API for real-time data flow to partner systems

Privacy Protections

  • No live audio streaming from sensors
  • Audio only captured on loud impulsive sounds (not voices)
  • Extended audio access (pre-2012) no longer available to police under current policy
  • Independent privacy audit by NYU Policing Project (July 2019): extremely low risk of voice surveillance
  • Approved by Oakland Privacy Advisory Commission (Nov 2019) and SF Privacy Advisory Board (2020)

Forensic Services

  • Detailed Forensic Reports (DFR) — court-admissible analysis
  • Expert witness testimony admitted in 400+ court cases in 25 states
  • 14 successful Frye challenges, 2 successful Daubert challenges

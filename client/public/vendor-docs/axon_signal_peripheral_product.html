Support in critical escalations

Axon Signal Sensor (formerly Signal Sidearm) fits seamlessly into your holster and wirelessly alerts nearby Axon cameras to begin recording the moment you draw your weapon.

Expanded holster compatibility for coverage across Uses of Force

Our first-of-its-kind partnership with Safariland, a leading global holster brand, expands Signal Sensor compatibility across a broader range of holsters. Together, we’re introducing integrated holster solutions not only for firearms but also for other Use of Force tools, including handcuffs, OC spray and batons—ensuring footage is captured in a range of critical incidents.

Critical evidence in the moment and after the fact

Hands-free activation helps ensure cameras are readily available for livestreaming for better situational awareness and real-time response, while footage is also documented for review and transparency after an incident.

Simplified management

Axon Device Manager makes setup easy with step-by-step guidance for quick calibration and assignment while over-the-air firmware updates through body-worn cameras keep devices current without extra administrative work.
